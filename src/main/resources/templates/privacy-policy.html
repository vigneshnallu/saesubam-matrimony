Privacy Policy

SaeSubam Matrimony - Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 & Google Play Compliance

Last Updated: September 2026 Back to Home

At SaeSubam Matrimony, we take your privacy and personal data protection with utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, process, and protect your personal information in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India) and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate matchmaking services, we collect personal details provided directly by candidate members or authorized parent/guardian handlers during account creation and profile setup:

  • Personal Identification: Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Age, Religion, Caste, Sub-Caste, Gothram, Rasi, Natchathiram, and Mother Tongue.
  • Contact Details: Mobile Number, Email Address, City, State, Native Place, and Residential Address.
  • Educational & Professional Details: Highest Education Degree, Occupation, Employment Sector, and Annual Income range.
  • Media & Documents: Profile Photos, Secondary Photos, Horoscope (Jathagam PDF/Image), and Payment Proof Screenshots.
  • Payment Information: UTR / Reference Transaction Numbers and uploaded payment proof screenshots (We do not store bank passwords or UPI PINs).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate matchmaking and verification purposes:

  • To display verified matrimonial candidate profiles to registered members seeking life partners.
  • To verify account identity via Email/Mobile OTP codes.
  • To process membership subscription upgrades (GOLD / PLATINUM) and manage contact view quotas.
  • To dispatch dual automated payment receipts and account verification alerts to your registered email.
  • To prevent fraudulent profiles, unauthorized commercial use, or safety violations.

3. Data Storage & Cloud Security

Your profile details and uploaded payment screenshots are securely stored using enterprise-grade Supabase PostgreSQL cloud databases with Row-Level Security (RLS) enabled. Photo and document uploads are protected with SSL/TLS encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party advertisers or telemarketing companies.

Your profile details (excluding contact mobile numbers and emails) are visible to registered candidate members. Full contact details are revealed only to subscribed members who have an active membership plan (GOLD / PLATINUM) and sufficient profile view quotas.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Rights

You have the right to request deletion or deactivation of your matrimony account at any time. Upon receiving an account deletion request, your candidate profile, photos, and associated data will be removed from active public view within 48 hours.
